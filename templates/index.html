Process

How FindIt works

Four simple steps from reporting to recovery — mapped directly to our project methodology.

1

Report

Log your lost or found item with description, category, date, location and a photo.

2

Search

Our system filters and matches reports by keywords, category and location.

3

Claim

Submit a claim with proof of ownership when a match is identified.

4

Recover

Admin verifies and hands over the item. Both parties get notified.
